## Verification decisions and ambiguities

1. **Percentage exercise 19 is underdetermined as printed.** It says the kit “includes” four categories but does not explicitly say they are the only categories. The intended textbook answer treats markers as the remainder: `100%-40%-15%-25%=20%`, then `20% of 80=16`. The website makes that assumption explicit in the question so the mathematics is honest.
2. **Percentage exercise 7 is repeating.** `4/12=1/3=33⅓%`. The stored numeric target `33.333%` is checked with tolerance, so reasonable decimal forms are accepted.
3. **Ratio page 2 uses blank numerators/denominators.** Visual inspection established their positions; P1-P12 preserve each equation and were independently cross-multiplied.
4. **Faucet:** 24 hours contains 48 half-hours, so `3×48=144`, matching the source.
5. **Aquarium:** `75,000 cm³=75 kg`; `35×75=2,625 g`. Ayana’s extension is `35×120=4,200 g`.
